What is LCG?

  1. The problem. When a sentence embeds a foreign phrase ("This station is 홍대입구"), zero-shot TTS speaks the phrase with the carrier language's accent — because classifier-free guidance applies one language condition to the whole utterance.
  2. The idea. Add a third guidance branch conditioned on the phrase's own language, and apply it only inside the phrase region.
  3. The trick. The phrase region is located by the model itself: we read its self-attention to find which audio frames align to the embedded text — no fine-tuning, no external aligner.

Overview

The challenge

Global guidance flattens the embedded phrase into the carrier accent (left); LCG steers only the phrase region toward its own language (right).

The method in three steps

  1. Probe — read decoder self-attention (layers 8 & 12) to find the frames aligned to the embedded phrase.
  2. Refine — union the masks from both language tags, then dilate for recall; the diffusion process absorbs the slack.
  3. Guide — add a phrase-language guidance term, gated by the mask, with its own strength λ.
